President Refshaw called meeting to order

Board members present: Mark Meurer, Zach Meinders, Scott Woolen, Kayla Inbody, and Nicole Jessen

Some members present in person and some on google meet.

**Secretary report:**  
Scott gave minutes of May board meeting, Lila Schmidt made motion to accept minutes.


**Treasurers report:**  
Jeremy gave report, beginning balance: $41,525.55 -1,146.23 for expenses and deposits of $773.  
Motion was made to accept and second the report.


**Sale committee:**  
NSIP online ram and ewe sale is done and Spencer NSIP sale is tomorrow the 25th.


**Newsletter committee:**  
Bev would like all info for the newsletter to her by September 21st.

**Ad committee:**  
Nothing to report


**Youth committee:**  
Adelyn was presented with her award for being the 2026 starter flock award winner. Adelyn gave a thank you to the association.


**Finance committee:**  
Audit is due in January.


**New business:**
* Honor Todd Taylor for being 2026 APSA honoree and give plaque to commemorate. Jeremy read the info submitted by Glen Jones, who
nominated Todd. Todd gave a talk and thanked the association for the honor.
* Ballots counted--1 ballot was returned, 1 vote for Bryce and 1 vote for Scott. These are your two new members. Scott was reelected for a second consecutive term and Bryce is elected back after being off for a bit. Jeremy thanked Mark for all his work on the board in the past.

**Old business:**  
New logo, if any member wants to submit an idea or design please do so.


**OPEN FLOOR TO ALL:**  
* There was some conversation on how the NSIP sale in Spencer came to be and how long some members have been involved in NSIP.
* Scott Bowdridge from West Virginia University gave an update on what his team has been doing for testing and research. They do a lot of traveling all over the country to accomplish this. He also mentioned that they are still wanting to get 3,000 polypay blood samples.
* Kayla had a question for the membership--is there any interest in a National sale?
* It was also noted that as of right now the Polypay breed has the second most number of animals entered in NSIP.

Adjourn--Zach made motion to adjourn and Caroline seconded.

Respectfully submitted,
Scott Wollin
